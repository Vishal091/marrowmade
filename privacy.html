Plain-language notice

Privacy, without the fine print theatre.

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This website is a static portfolio site. It does not intentionally run advertising pixels, analytics trackers, login accounts or a server-side database for project enquiry submissions.

Project enquiry form

When you submit the project enquiry form, the page prepares an email draft in your device's default email application. The website itself does not send or store the form contents. The draft is sent only if you choose to send it. Once sent, the message is handled by the email providers and inboxes involved.

External services

The site loads typography from Google Fonts and portfolio preview images from Thum.io, a third-party website screenshot service. When a browser requests those resources, those providers may receive standard connection information such as IP address, browser details and the requested resource. Please consult their own privacy notices for their handling of that data.

External websites

Portfolio links take you to third-party websites. Their content and privacy practices are outside Marrowmade's control.

Questions or requests

For privacy-related questions, use the studio's confirmed contact address once it is published. Do not send sensitive personal information through the portfolio form.
